# Invoica Communication Plan — Effective 2026-05-29

**Audience**: Crypto Twitter (CT), agent-economy builders, x402 ecosystem, AI infra developers.
**Voice**: technical, builder-respectful, no hype, no FUD, no competitor bashing, never platform-cliché.
**Brand discipline**: every post visibly Invoica. Branded imagery where possible (Invoica purple `#635BFF`, dark canvas, Inter typography). No off-brand bare-link posts.
**Agents authorized to post**: `invoica-x-admin` only (Grok-4). All other X agents retired 2026-05-29.

---

## Mission of the @invoica_ai handle

The Invoica X account exists to:

1. **Educate CT on the agentic economy** — what changes when AI agents earn, spend, invoice, and settle autonomously.
2. **Highlight other projects** — surface what builders in x402, agent payments, and adjacent infra are shipping. Generous, not territorial.
3. **Advocate the @godman-protocols open-spec family** — PACT, BOND, SCORE, LAX, SIGNAL, SOUL, AMF, DRS. Invoica composes on these; the X handle makes the case for why others should too.

The account is NOT for: pricing announcements during beta, roadmap promises, internal architecture leaks, controversial topics, or competitor attacks.

## Daily posting cadence

**One post per day minimum**, drawn from a rotating mix so the feed stays varied:

| Type | Cadence target | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Comment on an industry article** | 1-2× per week | QT the article URL + one substantive insight or counterpoint, not a hot take |
| **QT a known dev / builder** | 1-2× per week | Quote-tweet someone shipping in agent infra (Anthropic, OpenAI, Coinbase Dev, Tempo, Merit, Helixa, etc.) with a builder-mode reaction — never sycophantic |
| **Highlight an article / blog post** | 1× per week | Link a website article (Stripe Press, a16z, paradigm research, etc.) with the agent-economy lens |
| **Spotlight a project building on x402** | 1-2× per week | Shipping receipts > marketing — show what they actually built |
| **Educational thread** | 1× per 2 weeks | 4-6 tweet thread teaching a specific agent-economy concept (autonomous payments, signed mandates, on-chain reputation, etc.) |

**No more than one post per 4 hours** during normal cadence. No back-to-back posts.

## Ship-day cadence (when Invoica releases something material)

When Invoica ships something material (new feature, partner integration, SDK, protocol milestone), use the **technical + marketing combo**:

1. **Lead tweet** — what shipped, in one builder-mode sentence + a short demo asset (video, code snippet, screenshot). No emoji unless ironic.
2. **Follow-up thread** — 3-5 tweets unpacking the technical detail for builders who want substance. End with links (GitHub, npm, docs).
3. **QT amplification 24h later** — from the personal founder account, with one personal-frame line about why this matters strategically.
4. **Branded asset required**: video, screenshot, or 1080×1080 brand card. Never a bare URL.

Past ship-day examples (use as voice/structure references):
- PACT-Helixa thread (2026-05-28)
- @invoica/webhooks-receiver SDK launch (2026-05-29)

## Engagement rules

- **Reply to substantive responses within 4h** during waking hours (UTC 08:00-22:00).
- **Like + bookmark generous on builder content** in x402, agent payments, sovereign infra, godman-protocols mentions.
- **Do not chase trending hashtags** unless directly relevant to agent economy.
- **Do not respond to bait or ragebait** — silence is the response.
- **DMs**: handled by the founder personally; the X agent does not autonomously DM.

## Branded content discipline

Every post that goes out from `@invoica_ai`:

- Uses the word **Invoica** if referencing the company (never "we" without context).
- Visual content (images, videos, infographics) uses **Invoica purple `#635BFF`**, dark canvas, Inter typography (matches website and the PACT-Helixa video brand).
- Code snippets use mono font.
- Links are always to canonical surfaces (invoica.ai, docs.invoica.ai, npm, GitHub) — never tracking shorteners.
- No emoji in technical posts. Sparing emoji in educational threads only when functional (✓ ✗ → ↑ ↓).

## What NOT to post

- Pricing tier specifics (beta is free; pricing post-beta)
- Internal agent architecture, model providers, infra detail beyond what's already public
- Founder's personal name, personal handle, personal email, or personal phone number (per founder rule — privacy discipline)
- Competitor attacks (no Stripe, OpenAI, Anthropic, Coinbase punching)
- Speculative claims about partner roadmaps without partner sign-off
- Generic motivational content, "GM" posts, hype-coded language
